How results are determined

  • Workers are anchored on role detections (Groundman, Live-Line Worker, Foreman, Distribution Engineer, Safety Officer) or, when no role box covers them, on generic person detections. PPE is linked by head / torso / hands / feet region.
  • Each role has its own required PPE list. Each required item gets its own status. There is no numeric score: the overall result is the worst required-item status.
  • "Not detected" is not automatically non-compliant. It becomes POTENTIAL GAP only when the item is required, the model knows the class and the body region is clearly visible; otherwise NOT OBSERVABLE.
  • Box colour = model confidence: green counts as DETECTED; yellow / red on a required item = PENDING REVIEW.
  • PPE switched off in the config is not detected or scored; if a role requires it, it is listed under "Verify manually".
  • Project Shield is visual decision support. It does not replace field inspection by the supervisor / safety officer.
